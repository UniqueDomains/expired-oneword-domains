- `renewal_price`, Visible renewal price when available.
- `attractiveness`, Public composite naming band used as a decision-support signal.
- `demand`, Public buyer-pressure band when available.
- `length`, Character count without the TLD.
- `registrar`, Registrar name when known.
- `created_at`, Creation timestamp when known.
- `expires_at`, Expiry timestamp when known.

## ⚠️ Methodology and caveats

These are expired one-word domains pulled from 506 different TLDs, ranging from short brandable names to niche sector terms. Pricing skews wide: the bulk sit under $500, while a smaller group asks $10k or more, with a median ask of $3,961 across the full set. TLD coverage leans toward .it, .us, .store, .co, and .online, alongside app-focused extensions like .app and .site. Demand scoring shows most names in the low tier, but a meaningful slice — over 11,000 — score high or top 15%, which is where the sharper picks tend to sit.
